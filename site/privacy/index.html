Privacy Policy

The short version: we designed hexbase.dev so there is almost nothing to write a privacy policy about. No accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no ads. Your files are processed in your browser and never reach us — unless you explicitly click Share.

Effective date: July 18, 2026 · Contact: support@hexbase.dev

1. What happens in your browser, stays in your browser

Every tool on this site — the hex inspector, packet decoder, JSON and XML toolkits, timestamp converter and encoders — runs entirely in your browser tab as client-side code. Files you drop, tokens you paste and packet dumps you decode are read and parsed on your own machine. They are not uploaded, logged or seen by us. You can verify this in your browser's network inspector: using the tools generates no requests carrying your data. You can also read exactly what runs — the site is open source under the MIT license at github.com/davidapp/hexbase.dev.

Your theme preference (dark or light) is kept in your browser's localStorage under the key hexbase-theme. It never leaves your device and can be cleared like any site data.

2. The one exception: share links

Clicking Share uploads the current content so we can serve it as a short link. This is always an explicit action — nothing is shared automatically. When you create a share we store:

  • the content itself (up to 256 KB), in object storage on Cloudflare R2;
  • metadata: the share code, which tool it came from, the filename if one was provided, the content size, and the creation and expiry timestamps — in a Cloudflare D1 database.

Shares are not linked to you — we store no account, IP address or other identifier with them. Anyone who has the link can view the content, so don't share secrets. Every share expires after 30 days and is permanently deleted by a nightly cleanup job. Want one gone sooner? Email the link to support@hexbase.dev and we'll remove it.

3. Rate limiting without storing your IP

To keep the share feature free of abuse we count shares per client per hour. The counter key is a salted SHA-256 hash of your IP address combined with the current hour — the raw IP address is never written to storage, the hash cannot practically be reversed, and the counter itself expires within an hour.

4. Hosting and server logs

The site runs on Cloudflare Workers. Like any host, Cloudflare processes connection data (such as IP addresses and request headers) transiently to route traffic, terminate TLS and protect against attacks; see Cloudflare's privacy policy. We keep short-lived operational logs for debugging — error entries record the request path and error message, not who you are.

5. Cookies: none

The site sets no cookies — first-party or third-party. That's why there is no cookie banner: there is nothing to consent to.

6. No analytics scripts, no third parties — and honest page counting

There are no analytics scripts, advertising networks, tracking pixels, external fonts or third-party CDNs on this site. Every asset is served from hexbase.dev. The developer links directory points to external sites; once you follow a link, that site's own privacy policy applies.

To know which tools are worth improving, we count page views on the server, in aggregate: when a page is requested, the worker records the URL path and the request's country code into Cloudflare's Workers Analytics Engine. That's the entire record — no IP address, no user agent, no cookie, no user identifier, no client-side script. It can answer "the packet decoder was viewed 500 times from 30 countries this week" and nothing about you.

7. Your rights

Because we don't collect personal data through normal use of the site, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. If you believe a share link contains personal data about you — or you have any privacy question or complaint — email support@hexbase.dev and we will respond as quickly as we can, within the timelines required by the data-protection law that applies to you (such as the GDPR or CCPA).

8. Children

The site is a general-audience developer tool and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

9. Changes to this policy

If we ever add a feature that changes what data we handle, we will update this policy and its effective date before the feature ships. The current version always lives at hexbase.dev/privacy.

10. Contact

Privacy questions, removal requests, or anything else: support@hexbase.dev.
